How to Use Sensitive Field Validator

STEP 1

📂 Upload Data File

Navigate to Data Loading on the left sidebar. Choose your .csv file and click Load File. The system extracts columns and row samples for analysis.

STEP 2

🔍 Automatic Discovery

The discovery engine automatically scans headers and sample values to detect sensitive data types (PII, SSN, Credit Cards, Emails, Phone Numbers, Passwords, etc.).

STEP 3

📜 Review Audit Trail

Open History for past uploads and Audit Log for every pipeline stage, warning and rollback.

STEP 4

⚡ Plan Upgrades

Visit the Upgrade page to explore higher throughput tiers, custom compliance policies, and team management features.
